Point Software Reports 2026 Third Quarter Financial Results
Tel Aviv, Israel – Monday, November 2, 2026 – Check
Point® Software Technologies Ltd. (NASDAQ: CHKP), today announced its financial results for the quarter ended September 30th,
2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights:
•
Total Revenues: $670 million, a 1 percent decrease year over year
•
Security Subscriptions Revenues: $339 million, an 11 percent increase year over year
•
Remaining Performance Obligation (RPO)*: $2.5 billion, a 5 percent increase year over year.
•
GAAP Operating Income: $177 million, representing 26 percent of total revenues
•
Non-GAAP Operating Income: $251 million, representing 37 percent of total revenues
•
GAAP EPS: $1.82, a 45 percent decrease year over year. Excluding the one-time tax benefit recognized
in the same quarter last year, GAAP EPS increased 1 percent year over year.
•
Non-GAAP EPS: $2.49, a 37 percent decrease year over year. Excluding the one-time tax benefit recognized in
the same quarter last year, Non-GAAP EPS increased 1 percent year over year.
•
Cash Flow from Operations: $264 million, representing 39 percent of total revenues
•
Adjusted Free Cash Flow**: $258 million, representing 38 percent of total revenues
“Third-quarter results were in line with our expectations.
Security subscriptions revenue grew 11 percent year over year, supported by strong demand for Email, CTEM, and AI Security,” said
Nadav Zafrir, CEO of Check Point Software. “We believe revenues reached a trough in the third quarter, and we expect gradual improvement
beginning in the fourth quarter. The recovery is taking longer than anticipated, but we remain confident in our vision. Our leading indicators
are improving, which is encouraging, and we remain focused on driving sustainable growth.”
Financial Highlights Commentary
EPS: Earnings per diluted share in the third quarter of 2026
were $2.49 and $1.82 for Non-GAAP and GAAP, respectively. This compares to $3.94 and $3.28 in the third quarter of 2025. In connection
with a tax settlement signed during the third quarter of 2025, the company updated its tax provisions during that quarter, which resulted
in a benefit of approximately $1.47 to Non-GAAP and GAAP EPS.
Cash Balances, Marketable Securities & Short-Term Deposits:
$4,123 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to $2,817 million as of September 30, 2025. The increase in cash is primarily a
result of the $1.8 billion proceeds from our $2 billion convertible senior notes offering, net of issuance costs and net of purchased
capped calls. In the third quarter of 2025, the company executed a payment of approximately $160 million for the land of the new Check
Point Tel Aviv Campus.
Cash Flow: Cash flow from operations in the third quarter of
2026 was $264 million, which included $10 million in expense related to our currency hedging transactions. This compares to $241 million
in the third quarter of 2025, which included $3 million in benefit related to our currency hedging transactions.
Share Repurchase Program: During the third quarter of 2026,
the company repurchased approximately 2.5 million shares for a total of approximately $325 million. Over the last twelve months, we have
repurchased approximately 9 million shares.
Research & Development Grants: During the third quarter
of 2026, the company recognized a $25 million reduction in research and development expenses under Israel's new research & development
tax incentive program. The new Israeli law enacted on March 30, 2026, as part of the 2026 budget, provides tax incentives for research
and development activities carried out in Israel. Eligible Israeli companies within multinational groups can receive tax credits for qualifying
research & development expenses, which may be used to offset corporate taxes or minimum top-up tax, and unused credits may be converted
into cash grants after a defined period.
* Remaining Performance Obligation (RPO) is a measure that represents
the total value of non-cancellable contracted products and/or services that are yet to be recognized as Revenue as of September 30, 2026.
** Adjusted Free Cash Flow is a non-GAAP financial measure that
we define as Net Cash provided by operating activities, less purchases of property, equipment, Capitalization costs, and other assets,
net of Acquisition related costs.

About Check Point Software Technologies Ltd.
Check Point Software Technologies Ltd. (www.checkpoint.com) is a global cyber security leader protecting more than 100,000 organizations
worldwide. Its mission is to secure enterprises’ AI transformation. With a prevention-first approach and an open ecosystem architecture,
Check Point helps organizations block advanced threats, prioritize exposures, and automate security operations across complex digital
environments. The unified architecture simplifies protection across hybrid networks, multi-cloud environments, digital workspaces, and
AI systems. Structured around four strategic pillars, Hybrid Mesh Network Security, Workspace Security, Exposure Management, and AI Security,
Check Point delivers consistent protection and visibility across multivendor environments, enabling organizations to reduce risk, improve
efficiency, and accelerate innovation without increasing complexity.
